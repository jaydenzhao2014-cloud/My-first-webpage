🎮 HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Objective

Survive and eliminate all enemies to win! Stay inside the safe zone or take damage.

⌨️ Controls

  • WASD - Move your character
  • Mouse - Aim your weapon
  • Left Click - Shoot (hold for auto-fire)
  • 1-5 Keys - Switch weapons
  • E Key - Pick up items

🔫 Weapons

  • 1 - Pistol: Balanced, reliable
  • 2 - Shotgun: High damage, close range
  • 3 - Rifle: Fast fire rate
  • 4 - Sniper: Long range, high damage
  • 5 - SMG: Very fast, spray & pray

⚠️ Survival Tips

  • Stay inside the green circle (safe zone)
  • The safe zone shrinks over time
  • Pick up health packs to heal
  • Use stickers to communicate!
